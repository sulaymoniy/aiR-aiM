Findings

        
    
          
  • Artboard Coach model, 3181 × 2000, head and upper body on a dark backdrop baked into the artboard.
    • 
          
  • State machine State Machine 1 has exactly two inputs, both numbers:
            lips sync id (default 9) and timestamp (default 25). It runs four layers whose states report as
            emotion 1/emotion 2, the lip states, idle, and Timeline 2.
    • 
          
  • timestamp is not a time. It is the blend speed of the lip layer: 0 freezes the mouth on whatever shape it has,
            20 ≈ 170 ms to reach a shape, 30 ≈ 120 ms, 50 ≈ 70 ms, 100+ is instant. The app uses 45 while talking and 22 when settling to rest.
    • 
          
  • emotion is a layer, not an input. The layer flips between emotion 1 (neutral) and
            emotion 2 (heavy lids, flat lowered brows) every 4 s by itself, whatever the inputs are. The app re-applies
            emotion 1 each frame and blends emotion 2 in by the amount each emotion needs, then adds brows, eye size,
            gaze and head tilt procedurally (see the table in client/src/rig.ts).
    • 
          
  • "Timeline 29" does not exist: it is Timeline 2 followed by the next byte of the file ("9") when the binary is read as text,
            like "emotion 19" / "idle9". Timeline 2 is a 0.5 s ping-pong that slightly lowers the upper eyelids (a soft eye flutter),
            running constantly on its own layer. It is not a blink; the app adds real blinks.
    • 
          
  • idle: 0.67 s ping-pong head bob (~16 units). demo lipsync: a 2 s talking loop, played when
            lips sync id is 9 (8 shows the same). X and Y: no visible effect (empty timelines).
    • 
          
  • The eyelid rig (Ctrl_upper eyelid / Ctrl_lower eyelid root bones) only reaches the right eye, so blinks scale the
            eyes group instead.
